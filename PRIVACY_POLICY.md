# Privacy Policy

Glasses Finder processes shopping criteria users choose to provide, such as budget, face shape, frame width, style, prescription type, and lens preferences. It does not request payment information, passwords, government identifiers, precise location, or medical records.

Request details are used to generate recommendations and maintain basic security and debugging logs. Affiliate clicks may include the app, merchant, product, rank, and a non-personal click reference for attribution.

Glasses Finder may earn commissions through partner links. Recommendations rank user fit first. Contact: sholtsman29@gmail.com.
